The reader supports either loose fs*-org*.mat files or Indiv_organoids.zip,
including nested folders. You do not have to extract the archive first.
Identical zipped/extracted copies are not counted twice. Different content
for the same organoid ID is flagged as a conflict.
It reads individual-organoid spike/amplitude arrays, not raw waveforms.
Population result MAT files and MATLAB scripts are not executed.
The included hourly_stage.py supplies shared feature definitions only; importing
it does not start FinalSpark processing.

REAL-FILE VERIFICATION
All 45 public individual-organoid MAT files were tested. Their event counts
were independently checked against source HDF5 cell-array lengths.
45 recordings, 16 fs experiment prefixes, 856988 detected events.
Per-file declared durations: 200, 400, 600, 1000, 1200, 1800, 3600 seconds.
These differ from the general listing description. Each file's own metadata
is authoritative for the reader's duration-dependent summaries.
Per-organoid biological ages and death labels are unavailable in this schema.
No cohort age range or median is assigned to any individual recording.

METHODS AND LIMITS
The fixed-ISI channel-burst definition matches the implemented project reader:
at least five detections linked by intervals <=100 ms. This does not reproduce
all population-burst/criticality methods in the published MATLAB pipeline.
Rates divide by file-declared recording duration; continuous coverage is not
independently confirmed. Related experiment prefixes are retained in outputs.
This is descriptive external activity analysis, not validation of lifespan,
age prediction, causality, intelligence or an advantage over conventional AI.
