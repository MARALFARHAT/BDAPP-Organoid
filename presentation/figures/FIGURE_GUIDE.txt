INTERPRETATION RULES
- The FinalSpark age label is calendar time since batch creation.
- The endpoint label is time to a recorded experiment endpoint, not individual death.
- The added KCL target is days after dissociation, from five-minute well summaries.
- The positive benchmark does not validate intact-organoid biological age, lifespan,
  one-hour prediction, or transfer to FinalSpark.
- Rows, clock hours and repeated wells are not independent organoids.
- Missing exported values do not establish physiological silence.
- FinalSpark counts are not normalized by verified continuous exposure.
- DANDI rates use nominal source duration. Zenodo rates use declared file duration.
- DANDI rat cultures and Zenodo human organoid summaries remain descriptive tracks.
- Figure 05 describes the FinalSpark extractor. The KCL authors' feature definitions
  come from Axion / MEA-NAP and are not assumed identical.
- Figure 01 is a conceptual drawing. Figures 03-05, 12 and 19 explain methods or
  interpretation. Figure 06 uses actual public spike times; it is not a synthetic raster.
- No confidence interval or population-level biological validation is implied.
