INTERPRETATION
Richer features do not improve age transfer here. The two experiments cover
non-overlapping calendar ages and different environmental conditions.
Only two aggregate experiment records are available; biological independence
is unverified. The endpoints are activity-defined. These results do not
validate individual organoid lifespan prediction, neural intelligence, or
causal environmental effects. Hourly windows are temporally dependent.
